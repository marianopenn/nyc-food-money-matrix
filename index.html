    How we label places

    PE-backed

    A private-equity or growth-equity firm owns the brand or holds a reported stake, directly or through a holding company. That includes franchises: a Dunkin' may be run by a local franchisee, but the brand and its royalties sit with a buyout fund.

    Corp & VC

    Owned by a publicly traded company, or scaling on venture-capital rounds.

    Independent

    Run by its founders, a family or a local operator, with no institutional owner found in public reporting. Small local groups still count. Mixed cases are flagged in the notes.
