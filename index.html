PE Corp/VC Indie

    How we call it

    $ PE-backed

    A private-equity or growth-equity firm owns the brand or holds a reported stake, directly or through a holding company. That includes franchised chains: a Dunkin' may be run by a local franchisee, but the brand and its royalties sit with a buyout fund.

    C Corp & VC

    A publicly traded company owns it, or it's scaling on venture-capital rounds. That's big money with different incentives than a buyout fund.

    I Independent

    Run by its founders, a family or a local operator, with no institutional owner turned up in public reporting. A small local group like Andrew Tarlow's still counts. Mixed cases are flagged in the notes.
